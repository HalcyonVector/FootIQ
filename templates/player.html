What you get

What a player page shows

Passing and carrying
How far forward a player moves the ball by pass and by carry, measured from where the ball actually travelled, and how quickly they release it.
Aerial duels and hold-up play
Who wins headers, and what happens next: whether the ball stays with a teammate, goes to the opponent or goes out of play.
Decision making
Press resistance, finding space and creating shots, judged by what a pass gave the receiver and not just whether it was completed.
Final-third scores
Two scores for attacking play. Completeness is only high if a player is good at all four parts of it; Impact scores each touch on a player's two strongest parts.
Combination play
A player's most frequent passing partners, and what each partner does with the ball after receiving it.
Defending by zone
Actions in the box, the channels and the flanks are counted separately, because a centre-back's job is not a full-back's.
Tempo and transitions
Whether a player speeds the game up or settles it, and what they do in the two actions after winning the ball.
Compare players
Two to four players side by side, with a position-weighted rating and the full percentile breakdown.
Similar players
The players with the closest statistical profile across all leagues and seasons, which you can filter by age and league.

What you get

Event-Derived Analytics

Passing & Carrying Profiles
Progressive passes and carries measured from real ball movement, not FBref's box-score approximation — plus half-space activity and tempo control.
Aerial Duels & Hold-Up Play
Traces what happens after a header is won or a ball is held up — who keeps it, who loses it, and what it leads to.
Decision Making
Space-finding, press resistance, and shot creation — judged by what a pass buys the receiver, not just whether it was completed.
Final Third Composite
Completeness and Impact scores — a geometric-mean floor across four pillars, plus a per-touch score from a player's two strongest.
Combination Play
See a player's most-found teammates, and a pitch chart of exactly what that teammate does after receiving from them.
Zone-Based Defending
Box, channel, and flank actions kept separate — a center-back's job isn't a full-back's job, and the numbers shouldn't be blended together.
Tempo & Transitions
Who speeds the game up versus who settles it, and what a player does in the two actions right after winning the ball back.
Compare Any Players
Put 2-4 players side by side across every category, with a position-weighted Composite Rating and a full percentile breakdown.
Scout Similar Players
Find statistical twins for any player across all 5 leagues and 3 seasons, filtered by age and league — built for finding the next one, not just the current one.
